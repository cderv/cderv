I'm on the Quarto team at Posit. Quarto is an open-source system for publishing scientific and technical documents. I work mostly on [quarto-cli](https://github.com/quarto-dev/quarto-cli) and contribute to [the next version](https://github.com/quarto-dev/q2), a rewrite in Rust.

I also maintain the [quarto R package](https://github.com/quarto-dev/quarto-r) and several R Markdown packages, such as [rticles](https://github.com/rstudio/rticles) and [distill](https://github.com/rstudio/distill). With Yihui Xie and Emily Riederer, I co-wrote the [R Markdown Cookbook](https://pkg.yihui.org/rmarkdown-cookbook/).

I do most of my coding with AI agents, on Windows. When one of those tools breaks on Windows, I open an issue or send a fix.
